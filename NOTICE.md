# Third-party components

No third-party networking engine is included in this starter project.

**sing-box** (https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box) is a candidate external engine. It is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later, and its repository includes additional language concerning the use of its name and implication of association. Any eventual distribution must review and comply with its full current terms and licenses.

GTK4 and PyGObject are system-provided dependencies; respect their licenses when distributing packages.
